# 🧠 Team Health Check Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project simulates a **Team Health Check** model I designed as a Scrum Master to assess and continuously improve team dynamics and performance across multiple Sprints.

It helps Agile teams reflect on aspects like morale, clarity, collaboration, and quality, using anonymous feedback and clear scoring visuals.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Promote psychological safety by gathering honest team feedback
- Identify early signs of misalignment or burnout
- Use data-driven discussions in Sprint Retrospectives
- Track trends over time to ensure continuous improvement

---

## 📊 Metrics Measured

Each team member scores the team (0 to 5) in the following areas:

| Metric                | Description |
|-----------------------|-------------|
| 💬 Communication      | Are we openly sharing thoughts and ideas? |
| 😊 Morale             | How is the overall energy and motivation of the team? |
| 📅 Predictability     | Are we able to meet our Sprint commitments? |
| 🧠 Clarity            | Do we have a clear understanding of Sprint goals? |
| 🤝 Collaboration      | Are we working well across roles? |
| 🧪 Quality            | Are we maintaining good coding/testing practices? |

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Google Forms**: To collect anonymous feedback
- **Google Sheets / Excel**: For scoring and visualizing trends (charts & graphs)
- **Miro / FigJam (Optional)**: To present results visually in Retrospectives
